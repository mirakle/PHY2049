Welcome!

In this activity you will first watch what happens when a magnet moves near a coil connected to a light bulb, then explain it with magnetic flux and Lenz's law, and finally calculate induced emfs and currents.

How it works: 20 questions, 5 points each (100 points total). You get 3 attempts per question. A correct answer on any attempt earns 5 points; after 3 incorrect attempts the question earns 0 points and the solution is shown. The next question appears once the current one is finished. Numbers change every time you start, so your answers are your own.

Question types: multiple choice, select-all, fill in the blank, ranking, hotspots (click a part of a figure), and drawing on the screen. Every drawing question has a keyboard alternative.

Simulation: magnet, coil and light bulb

Drag the magnet (or the coil) with the mouse or a finger, or use the sliders and buttons below. The viewer on the right looks at the coil along its axis; the area vector of the coil points toward that viewer.
